01/04 Conversational AI × Telephony

AI Voice Agents

AI receptionists that answer a company’s phone like its best receptionist would. In production at many companies.

  • 0.85 s Median response time, from the moment the caller stops speaking
  • 12,600+ Calls answered since July
  • 96% Correct transfers in blind tests, the best of every variant tested
View case studyView case study

  1. The call comes in through the company’s phone system (a Yeastar P-Series PBX) and the assistant picks up.

  2. Best-in-class speech recognition and synthesis carry the voice. Every turn passes through my own orchestration layer, which supervises the model in real time and fails over between AI providers.

  3. The layer enforces the steps of the process and applies deterministic rules: who the call goes to, what gets confirmed and how a number is read back.

  4. The message is read back, confirmed and delivered by email or WhatsApp to the right person, or the call is put through. At any hour.
